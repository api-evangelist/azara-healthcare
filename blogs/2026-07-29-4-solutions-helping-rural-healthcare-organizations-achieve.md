The Rural Health Transformation Program (RHTP) represents one of the most significant investments in rural healthcare in decades. Across the country, states are investing in initiatives designed to improve access, strengthen workforce capacity, modernize technology, and create sustainable models of care that improve outcomes for rural communities. But funding alone doesn't create transformation.
