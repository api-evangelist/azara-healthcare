For organizations managing risk-based contracts, risk adjustment factor (RAF) gap closure is both a reporting challenge and an operational imperative. The scale of the opportunity is significant: CMS estimated that the Medicare Advantage risk-score trend would account for more than $13 billion in additional plan payments in 2027 , influenced in part by diagnosis coding patterns. Success depends on more than identifying RAF opportunities.
